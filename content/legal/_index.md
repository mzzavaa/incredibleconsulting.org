Two documents, held once in the shared theme and rendered on every site in the
family, so a correction is made in one place rather than seven.

- [**Impressum**](/legal/imprint/). The disclosure required under the Austrian
  E-Commerce-Gesetz, Mediengesetz, Gewerbeordnung and Unternehmensgesetzbuch.
  Deliberately in German: the obligations are Austrian and the German text is the
  one that discharges them.
- [**Privacy**](/legal/privacy/). What this site does with your data, which is
  nothing, with an English summary at the end.

## The short version of the privacy notice

This is a static site. No cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no tag
manager, no tracking pixels, and no forms, so nothing is submitted to us. No
external stylesheets, fonts or scripts either, so your browser makes no
third-party requests while reading it. Outbound links are followed only when you
click them, and the receiving provider's terms then apply.

**If any of that changes, the privacy notice is wrong and gets updated first.**
Adding an analytics snippet, a hosted font, an embedded video or a signup form
all change what has to be disclosed, and most of them also require a consent
banner.

## About this domain

This domain exists to catch a common misspelling and to send people to the right
place. It has no forms, no analytics and no tracking, and it is linked from
nowhere. The notices above are here because Austrian law requires them on a
website, not because this one does anything with your data.
