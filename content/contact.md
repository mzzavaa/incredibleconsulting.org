**Purpose.** Catch the visitor who arrived by typo, could not tell which site they wanted, and just wants to reach a person. One address, one booking link, no form.

**Contains.**
- Group email as the single fallback address, with a line on what to expect back
- Direct booking link for a short intro call, for anyone whose question is really a consulting question
- LinkedIn and GitHub as the two public profiles worth listing
- One line pointing anyone with a commercial request to the canonical consulting site instead, so enquiries do not queue up on a defensive domain
- Explicit note that this domain runs no forms, no tracking, and no newsletter signup

**Primary CTA.** Email the group address, or book the intro call if the question is about an engagement.
